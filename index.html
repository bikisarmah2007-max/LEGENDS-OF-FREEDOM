About Leh-Ladakh

Leh-Ladakh is a stunning Union Territory in northern India. Famous for its dramatic landscapes, rugged mountain passes, and vibrant Tibetan Buddhist culture, Leh is a dream destination for adventure seekers and cultural explorers alike. Nestled between the Great Himalayan, Karakoram, Zanskar, and Ladakh mountain ranges.
